How To Play

Move with arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen pad. Swipe on touch screens.
Eat fruit to grow longer and score points.
Avoid the walls, your own body, and every other snake's body.
Battle computer-controlled snakes hunting the same fruit. Pick how many from Opponents in the menu.
When any snake dies, its body turns into fruit for the survivors.
Win a level by outlasting every opponent to unlock the next one. Levels go on forever, so keep climbing! Press space to pause.
